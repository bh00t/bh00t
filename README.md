## The short version

Spent a decade making data move reliably at scale — ingestion, transformation, and reporting pipelines on AWS for enterprises that can't afford for them to break. Now I'm taking that same engineering discipline and aiming it at the most interesting problem in software today: **getting AI to actually do things, not just talk about them.**

## Where my focus is

🧠 **Agentic AI** — agents that plan, call tools, and follow through on multi-step goals. The frontier I find genuinely exciting.

🛠️ **AI-driven development** — shipping real, working software with AI in the loop. Faster iteration, smaller gap between idea and demo.

🔗 **AI × Data** — my unfair advantage. Most people learning agents have never run a production pipeline; most data engineers haven't touched agents. I'm building where the two meet.
