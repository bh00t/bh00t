### 🙋 Who am I

Data & Integration Engineer with **10+ years** on AWS — but these days I'm most excited about **AI agents** and **agentic systems**. I'm drawn to the idea of software that doesn't just respond, but *reasons, plans, and gets things done on its own.*

My edge: I bring production data engineering discipline to AI — most people exploring agents have never shipped a real pipeline, and most data engineers haven't touched agents yet. I'm building at that intersection.
